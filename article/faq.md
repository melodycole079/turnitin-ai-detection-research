# Turnitin AI Detector FAQ

This FAQ is a short companion to the [student guide](turnitin-ai-detector-guide-2026.md).

- **Can a score alone prove misconduct?** No. Turnitin says AI-writing results require human judgment and institutional policy.
- **Is there one acceptable similarity percentage?** No. Expectations depend on the assignment, instructor, and institution.
- **Should I use a humanizer to change my paper?** No. It is not a guaranteed detector solution and may violate course policy or damage meaning.
- **What evidence should I keep?** Keep outlines, notes, drafts, citations, version history, and feedback.
- **Is the benchmark a Turnitin replica?** No. It is an independent classifier benchmark with a transparent baseline.
